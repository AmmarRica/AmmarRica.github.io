How to play

  • Goal: fill the grid to reveal a hidden picture.
  • Clues above each column and beside each row list the runs of filled squares in order — e.g. 3 1 means a block of 3, a gap, then 1.
  • Fill mode paints squares; Mark mode places an ✕ on squares you know are empty. Tap the toggle to switch.
  • Swipe across cells to fill or mark a whole line at once.
  • A clue greys out once its block is placed. A full row/column dims when it matches.
  • Use 💡 Hint to reveal a square (+10s), ↶ to undo, ⟳ to reset.
  • Every puzzle has exactly one solution — no guessing needed.
