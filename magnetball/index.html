A fast physics soccer game — built for touch.

How to Play

🎯 Goal. Push the ball into the opponent's net. Most goals before the clock runs out wins.

🕹️ Move. Touch and drag anywhere on the right half — a joystick appears under your thumb. Drag to steer; further = faster.

👟 Kick. Hold the KICK pad (bottom-left) with your other thumb. You kick the instant you touch the ball, once per press — release and press again to kick again.

⚡ Dribble. Nudge the ball along with your body as you run, then tap KICK to fire it — it plays like a free puck, so keep it in front of you.

👥 2-Player. Lay the phone flat between you. Player 1 uses the bottom controls, Player 2 the top (rotated) controls.

🎮 Controllers. Set Input to Controllers and connect gamepads (press a button to wake them). Each pad takes a seat — you first, then an opponent, then a teammate — so 2 pads is 1v1, 8 pads is a full 4v4. Left stick moves, A / RT kicks. Empty seats stay bots.
